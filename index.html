Data comes from each county's Tax Commissioner or Sheriff website (linked on every county tab) and is checked weekly. Addresses are placed on the map with the U.S. Census geocoder; county boundaries are from the Census cartographic files. Georgia counties publish very different amounts of detail, so some tabs have full results and others only links. Lists change up to the day of the sale; always confirm with the county.
